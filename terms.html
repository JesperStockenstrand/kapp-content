Who can use Kapp

You must be at least 16 years old. You can race as a guest or sign in with Apple or Google. Keep your account to yourself; you're responsible for what happens with it.

Your safety comes first

You run at your own risk. Kapp is a game, not a coach or a medical service. Follow traffic rules, watch your surroundings, and don't look at the screen when it isn't safe; spoken updates are there so you don't have to. Run at a pace that's right for you and stop if you feel unwell. Check with a doctor before you start if you're unsure whether running is right for you.

Fair play

  • Run your races yourself, on foot.
  • Don't use fake-location apps, vehicles, or anything else that misreports your distance. Results with a fake location don't count.
  • Don't interfere with Kapp, other runners or our servers, and don't try to read data you're not meant to see.

We can void results, reset ratings, or suspend or close accounts that break these rules.

Races and results

Distances come from your phone's GPS and are estimates; tunnels, buildings and trees can affect them. Places and records are based on the data we receive. Virtual runners ("bots") may fill quiet races on your phone; they're always marked and never count for places or records.
