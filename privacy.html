Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: Kapp uses your phone's GPS to measure how far you run, and that position never leaves your phone. Other runners only see a made-up name, the flag you chose and how far you've got. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. You can delete your account and its data from the app at any time.

Who is responsible

Kapp is built and run by Jesper Stockenstrand, Sweden, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: kapp.app@stockenstrand.com.

Your location stays on your phone

During a race, Kapp reads your phone's GPS (also with the screen locked, so the race keeps counting) to measure the distance you cover. Coordinates are never sent to our servers or to anyone else. Only the resulting numbers are shared: distance in metres and your current pace. Kapp only uses location while a race is running.

What we store, and why

  • An account ID. As a guest you get an anonymous ID; if you sign in with Apple or Google, our sign-in provider (Firebase Authentication) also holds your email address. We don't keep your name: we don't ask Apple for it, and the name Google shares is removed from your account right after you sign in. We don't show your email to anyone, and we don't store it in race data.
  • Race data: which race you joined (distance, terrain, start time), your distance and pace during it, your finish time, the flag you chose to show (a country code, or none), and the pace range you picked at setup.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a push token for your device and which notifications you want (the 15-minute reminder, the race start). With both turned off, no token is sent.
  • Fair play: whether your phone reported a fake-location app during a race. Such a result doesn't count, and repeated cases are kept for review.
  • Matchmaking: a performance rating per terrain, worked out from your own race times, so you race people of similar speed. It is never shown to you or anyone else. We also keep how many of your first three "placement" races you've done.
  • With an account: your race history, totals, streak and personal records, and whether you have Premium.

Your race settings (distance, units, spoken updates, flag) are also stored on your phone.

What other runners see

In a race, other runners see a name generated from your account ID (for example "SwiftFox42"), your flag if you chose to show one, and your distance, pace, place and finish time. They never see your email, your real name or where you are.

Legal basis

  • Providing the service (contract): running races, results, history and your account.
  • Legitimate interest: fair matchmaking, keeping results honest and preventing abuse.
  • Consent: location and notification permissions, which you give in your phone's settings and can withdraw at any time.

Where data is processed

We use Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.) as our processor: the database is in Stockholm, Sweden, and the server functions run in Belgium. Push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's push service. Signing in with Apple or Google is handled by those companies under their own privacy policies. If you buy Premium, payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play; we only learn whether you have Premium.

We don't sell data, and we don't use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

How long we keep it

  • A place in the queue is deleted when the race is formed.
  • Race results are kept so that everyone's results stay complete.
  • Account data, history and your rating are kept until you delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. After you confirm with Apple or Google, your account and everything linked to it are deleted: profile, history, records, streak, rating and race assignments. In races you already ran, your result stays for the other runners, but under a new random name and without any link to you, your push token or your flag.

A guest account is tied to the app on your phone. To have a guest's data removed, contact us with the name shown in the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and receive your data in a portable format. Write to kapp.app@stockenstrand.com. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or the authority where you live.

Age

Kapp is for people aged 16 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children; if you believe a child has used Kapp, contact us and we'll delete the data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the app will tell you.
