Common questions

How does a race work?

Tap "I'm in" any time before five to the hour. At :55 we put you in a group of runners at your distance and speed, and at the full hour the clock starts for everyone. Run anywhere outdoors; the first to cover the distance wins.

Does Kapp share where I am?

No. GPS is used on your phone to measure the distance, and only the distance and pace are shared. Other runners see a made-up name, your flag if you chose one, and how far you've run.

My distance stopped counting.

Check that location is on for Kapp, set to "While using the app" with precise location. Open sky works best; tunnels, dense trees and tall buildings can block the signal for a while. Keep Kapp open during the race (the screen may be locked).

My result says "not counted".

Your phone reported a fake-location (mock location) app during the race, so the result doesn't count for places, wins, your rating or records. If you didn't use one on purpose, turn off any mock location app in your phone's developer settings before the next race. If you think it's a mistake, write to us with the time of the race.

What are placement races?

Your first three completed races on a terrain help us find runners of your speed. After that, the pace you actually run decides your group.

What are the runners marked BOT?

Virtual runners that fill quiet races, only on your phone. They never count for places or records.

Notifications aren't arriving.

Check Profile → Settings → Notifications in Kapp, and that your phone allows notifications for Kapp in its system settings.

How do I keep my history on a new phone?

Sign in with Apple or Google (Profile → Keep your progress). On the new phone, sign in with the same account. Guests have no history to move.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. You'll confirm with Apple or Google, and then your account and its data are deleted. If you have Premium, cancel the subscription in the App Store or Google Play first.
